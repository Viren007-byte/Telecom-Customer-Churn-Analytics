# Business insights and recommendations

This is **synthetic** data. Observed relationships are descriptive and should not be presented as causal findings.

## Questions to investigate
1. Which contract type has the highest churn rate? Compare both churn **rate** and churn **count**.
2. Are customers in their first year more likely to churn?
3. Which internet service and payment methods have elevated churn?
4. How much monthly recurring revenue is associated with churned customers?
5. Do customers without tech support or online security show higher churn?

## Suggested actions to evaluate (not proven by this dataset)
- Test onboarding interventions for new customers.
- Test longer-term plan incentives among month-to-month customers.
- Investigate billing friction for electronic-check users.
- Pilot targeted support outreach and measure retention with a controlled experiment.

## Metric definitions
- **Churn rate:** churned customers / total customers in the synthetic sample.
- **Monthly revenue lost:** sum of monthly charges for customers labeled churned; a snapshot proxy, not measured realized revenue loss.
- **Monthly revenue:** sum of monthly charges across the sample, not actual booked revenue.
